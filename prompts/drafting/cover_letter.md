## Structure

1. One sentence: which programme and why now.
2. One paragraph: what the project is and how it answers the funder's
   stated priority (echo their own words once).
3. One paragraph: why this artist for this opportunity — anchor in one
   concrete prior work, not a list.
4. One sentence: closing offer (e.g., availability for interview, link to
   sample documentation).

Avoid "I am writing to apply" and other openers that waste a sentence.
