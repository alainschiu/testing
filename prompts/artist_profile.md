## The Drawing Room Project (load-bearing pitch)

Practice-led research into loneliness as a *directed* sonic condition; the
nocturne / drawing-room miniature / slow chamber movement as interiority
turned outward in hope of being heard, and the political determination of how
that yearning is received. Working title: *The Drawing Room as Listening
Space: Nocturne, Loneliness, and the Situated Listener under Hypermediation.*

Anchors: Chion (modes of listening), Wittgenstein (private language),
Kierkegaard, Hegel ("die Innigkeit"), Baudrillard, Voegelin, Kane,
Jankélévitch, Gallope, Forensic Architecture / Lawrence Abu Hamdan (*Saydnaya*
as the political inverse anchor).

## Career destination (7–10 years)

Full-time HK or overseas academic post with contractual research time. The
